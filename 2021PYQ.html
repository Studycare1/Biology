Section B: Subjective Questions & Answers

Short Answer Questions (2 Marks)

Q1. जीएमओ पर प्रकाश डालें। (Throw light on GMOs)
Genetically Modified Organisms (GMOs) are plants, animals, or microorganisms whose genetic material has been altered using genetic engineering techniques to introduce desired traits. E.g., Bt Cotton.
Q2. मानव अंडाशय के अनुप्रस्थ काट का चित्र बनाएँ। (Draw TS of human ovary)
A labelled diagram showing Germinal epithelium (outer layer), Tunica albuginea, Stroma, Primary follicles, Secondary follicles, and Mature Graafian follicle.
Q3. अमीबियासिस क्या है? (What is Amoebiasis?)
Amoebiasis is an intestinal infection caused by the protozoan parasite Entamoeba histolytica. Symptoms include abdominal pain, severe diarrhea (often with blood and mucus), and cramps.
Q4. एलर्जी क्या है? (What is Allergy?)
Allergy is the exaggerated/hypersensitive response of the immune system to certain harmless foreign substances (allergens). Symptoms include sneezing, watery eyes, runny nose, skin rashes, and difficulty breathing.
Q5. प्रतिरक्षी का चित्र बनाएँ। (Draw diagram of primary antibody)
A Y-shaped structure showing two heavy (H) chains and two light (L) chains connected by disulfide bonds. It has variable regions at the tips for antigen binding and a constant region at the base.
Q6. जैव-विविधता हॉटस्पॉट क्या है? (What is a biodiversity hotspot?)
A biogeographic region with a significant reservoir of biodiversity that is under threat from humans. Two hotspots in India are: 1. Western Ghats, 2. Eastern Himalayas. Features: High endemism and high species richness.
Q7. रक्त के कार्यों को लिखें। (Write functions of blood)
1. Transport of oxygen, nutrients, and hormones to cells. 2. Transport of waste products (CO2, urea) to excretory organs. 3. Regulation of body temperature and pH. 4. Protection against infections via WBCs.
Q8. ग्लोबल वार्मिंग क्या है? (What is global warming?)
Global warming is the long-term heating of Earth's climate system due to human activities, primarily fossil fuel burning, which increases heat-trapping greenhouse gas levels. Effects include melting glaciers, rising sea levels, and extreme weather events.
Q9. अंत: प्रजनन क्या है? (What is internal fertilization?)
Internal fertilization is the union of an egg and sperm cell during sexual reproduction inside the female body. Example: Humans, reptiles, birds.
Q10. क्लाइनफेल्टर सिंड्रोम पर प्रकाश डालें। (Klinefelter syndrome)
A genetic condition in males where they have an extra X chromosome (47, XXY). Symptoms include tall stature, reduced muscle mass, enlarged breasts (gynecomastia), small testicles, and infertility.
Q11. परखनली शिशु किसे कहते हैं? (What is a test-tube baby?)
A test-tube baby is a baby conceived through In Vitro Fertilization (IVF), a process where an egg is fertilized by sperm outside the body in a laboratory dish, and the resulting embryo is then implanted into the uterus.
Q12. मरुस्थलीय पौधों के अनुकूलन (Desert plant adaptations)
Desert plants (Xerophytes) have: 1. Thick cuticle to prevent water loss. 2. Sunken stomata. 3. Leaves reduced to spines (e.g., cactus). 4. Fleshy stems for water storage. 5. Deep roots to absorb water.
Q13. अल्कोहल के दुष्परिणामों का वर्णन करें। (Ill effects of alcohol)
Alcohol consumption leads to liver cirrhosis, brain damage (impaired coordination, memory loss), addiction, stomach ulcers, and social/economic ruin of the individual and family.
Q14. अंतर्जात प्रतिरक्षा पर प्रकाश डालें। (Innate immunity)
The specific resistance present from birth that prevents the entry of pathogens into our body is called innate immunity. It has four types: (i) Physical barrier, (ii) Physiological barrier, (iii) Cellular barrier, (iv) Cytokine barrier.
Q15. संक्षेप में ट्रांसक्रिप्शन का वर्णन करें। (Describe transcription)
Copying of genetic information from one strand of DNA into mRNA is called transcription. In eukaryotic cells, it occurs in the nucleus, where adenosine and uridine pair with thymine and guanine of the DNA template.
Q16. बायोपाइरेसी का संक्षिप्त विवरण (Biopiracy)
Using biological resources without the proper authorization or compensation of the nation or related people is called biopiracy. It is often done by multinational companies or organizations.
Q17. मेंडल की सफलता के कारण (Reasons for Mendel's success)
(i) Pea seeds were easily available. (ii) Pea flowers are bisexual, allowing easy self-pollination. (iii) The plant is small and easy to grow. (iv) The characters of the pea plant are clearly visible, making observation easy.
Q18. युग्मन और प्रतिकर्षण को परिभाषित करें। (Coupling and Repulsion)
Coupling: When two dominant alleles or two recessive alleles come from the same parent and stay together. Repulsion: When two dominant alleles or two recessive alleles come from different parents and remain separate.
Q19. DNA प्रतिकृति के लिए आवश्यक दो एन्जाइम (Enzymes for DNA replication)
(i) DNA Polymerase: Catalyzes the polymerization of deoxyribonucleotides using a DNA template. (ii) DNA Ligase: Joins the DNA fragments together.
Q20. सूक्ष्मप्रवर्धन क्या है? (What is micropropagation?)
The method of producing thousands of plants from a small amount of tissue through tissue culture is called micropropagation. Advantage: Plants with desired genetic traits can be obtained in a very short time.

Long Answer Questions (5 Marks)

Q21. ऊतक संवर्धन पर एक निबंध लिखें। (Essay on Tissue Culture)
When a new plant is produced from a specific part of a plant (root, stem, leaf) in a controlled medium, it is called tissue culture. It requires controlled light, temperature, humidity, and pH. It is based on the principle of Totipotency. Initially, coconut water or yeast extract was used as a medium, but now a special nutrient solution is used. Three main pathways are: (i) By apical meristem (produces virus-free plants), (ii) By adventitious bud, (iii) By adventive embryony.
Q22. पुष्पीय पौधों में निषेचन की क्रिया (Fertilization in plants)
The union of male and female gametes is called fertilization. In angiosperms, this occurs through double fertilization. The pollen tube enters the ovule and discharges two male gametes. One fuses with the egg to form the zygote (syngamy), and the other fuses with the two polar nuclei to form the endosperm (triple fusion).
Q23. रिकॉम्बिनेंट डीएनए तकनीक (Recombinant DNA technique)
Recombinant DNA technology involves manipulating genetic material to create new combinations of genes. It requires restriction enzymes (to cut DNA), a vector (to carry DNA), ligase (to join DNA), and a host organism. It is used in gene mapping, developmental studies, antisense therapy, and producing GMOs.
Q24. तीन पारिस्थितिक पिरामिडों का वर्णन (Ecological Pyramids)
1. Pyramid of Numbers: Shows the number of individuals at each trophic level. (Upright in grassland, inverted in a tree ecosystem). 2. Pyramid of Biomass: Shows the total dry weight of organisms at each level. (Upright in terrestrial, inverted in aquatic). 3. Pyramid of Energy: Shows the flow of energy. It is always upright.
Q25. जनसंख्या नियंत्रण हेतु गर्भ निरोधन की विधियाँ (Contraceptive methods)
1. Barrier methods: Condoms, diaphragms. 2. Hormonal methods: Oral pills (prevent ovulation). 3. IUDs: Copper-T, Loop (prevent implantation). 4. Surgical methods: Vasectomy in males (cutting vas deferens) and Tubectomy in females (cutting fallopian tubes).
Q26. मत्स्य पालन क्या है? (Aquaculture/Fish farming)
Rearing and breeding of aquatic animals (fish, prawns) for food is called aquaculture. Role in food quality: Fish is a rich source of high-quality animal protein, omega-3 fatty acids, and essential vitamins. It helps in combating malnutrition and improving overall dietary quality.
